Pure Intervals Privacy Policy

Pure Intervals is designed with your privacy as a top priority. We do not collect, store, or share any of your personal data.

Pure Intervals is owned and operated by Tres Arboles Ltd.

Local Storage Only

All of your workout data, settings, and history are stored exclusively on your device. Nothing is ever uploaded to our servers or any third-party service.

If you delete the app, all your data is permanently removed from your device.

No Internet Required

Pure Intervals works entirely offline. The app does not make any network requests to send or receive your personal data. Your workouts stay on your device and yours alone.

No Tracking or Analytics

We do not use any analytics services, tracking pixels, advertising identifiers, or fingerprinting technologies. Your usage of the app is completely private and unmonitored.

No Account Required

Pure Intervals does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

No Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties. Since we do not collect any data in the first place, there is nothing to share.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, they are generated and delivered locally on your device to alert you about workout interval changes. No notification data is sent externally.

Policy Changes

If we ever update this privacy policy, changes will be reflected in this section of the website and on the corresponding screen within the app. Our commitment to not collecting your data will not change.
