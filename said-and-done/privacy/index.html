Said & Done Privacy Policy

Said & Done is designed with your privacy as a top priority. We do not collect, store, or share any of your personal data.

Said & Done is owned and operated by Tres Arboles Ltd.

Your Data Stays Yours

Your tasks, lists, and settings are stored on your device. Tres Arboles does not operate any servers that receive your data, and we have no way to see, read, or recover what you put in the app.

Optional iCloud Sync

You can choose to sync your data across your Apple devices using iCloud. Sync is optional, and the app works without it.

If you turn sync on, your data is stored in your own private iCloud account, which is provided by Apple and governed by Apple's terms and privacy policy. Tres Arboles cannot access data stored in your iCloud account.

Voice to Tasks

When you add tasks by voice, Said & Done uses features built into your device and provided by Apple to turn what you say into tasks:

  • On devices that support Apple Intelligence, the app uses Apple Intelligence to understand what you say and create tasks from it.
  • On devices that do not support Apple Intelligence, the app uses Apple's speech recognition to convert your speech to text.

Tres Arboles never receives, stores, or has access to your audio, transcripts, or tasks.

Because these features are provided by Apple, Apple may process your voice input on your device or, depending on your device and settings, on Apple's servers. We do not control that processing and cannot see it. It is governed by Apple's privacy policy, available at apple.com/privacy.

Voice entry requires your permission to use the microphone and speech recognition. You can withdraw that permission at any time in your device's Settings, and you can always add tasks by typing instead.

Reminders

If you set a reminder on a task, Said & Done schedules a notification for the date you chose. Reminders are created and delivered locally on your device, and require your permission to send notifications. No reminder or notification data is sent to Tres Arboles.

No Tracking, Analytics, or Diagnostics

We do not use any analytics services, diagnostic or crash reporting tools, tracking pixels, advertising identifiers, or fingerprinting technologies. Your usage of the app is completely private and unmonitored.

No Account Required

Said & Done does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

No Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties. Since we do not collect any data in the first place, there is nothing to share.

Deleting Your Data

You can delete tasks at any time within the app. If you delete the app, the data stored on that device is removed. If you used iCloud sync, data stored in your iCloud account remains under your control and can be removed through your device's iCloud settings.

Contacting Us

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message. We use that only to reply to you.

Policy Changes

If we ever update this privacy policy, changes will be reflected on this page. Our commitment to not collecting your data will not change.
